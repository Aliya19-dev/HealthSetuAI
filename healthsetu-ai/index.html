Sources & AI provenance

On-device AI (used)

Transformers.js with Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (Apache 2.0) for symptom flags, and paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2 (Apache 2.0) for multilingual semantic matching. Both run in your browser. The AI can only raise urgency.

Triage logic and score (used, not validated)

Hand-written rules inspired by MTS-style discriminators. It is not the licensed Manchester Triage System or NHS Pathways. The 0–100 score summarises the rules plus severity, duration and age. The colour is set by the rules, not by the number. A clinician must review this before real use.

Search (used)

MiniSearch (MIT) BM25 over scheme entries, re-ranked by embedding similarity when the AI is loaded.

Maps, facilities and specialist search (used)

Nearby places and specialist searches use the Mapbox Search Box API, and the optional map uses Mapbox GL JS, with your own Mapbox public token (Mapbox has a free tier; check its current limits and terms). Your approximate location and the search words are sent to Mapbox to find places, and nothing else. Mapbox coverage of Indian health facilities has not been verified, so lists may be thin. Directions open in Google Maps. The Goa referral names (GMC Bambolim, Hospicio Margao, Asilo Mapusa) are shown without phone numbers or coordinates because those were not verified. You can load an official directory CSV (name,tier,lat,lon,phone) on the Results screen.

Government schemes (starter text, unverified)

Short summaries of PM-JAY, DDSSY, eSanjeevani, JSY and Jan Aushadhi written from general knowledge. Official sources: pmjay.gov.in, esanjeevani.mohfw.gov.in, nhm.gov.in, janaushadhi.gov.in, and the Goa Directorate of Health Services. The MyScheme portal (myscheme.gov.in) is a good place to check criteria. Load your verified JSON to replace the starter data.

Datasets named in the brief (not bundled)

OGD India / Kaggle health centre directories and MyScheme datasets: I could not confirm these exist as described, so nothing is bundled from them. Check the exact dataset and its license before use.

Indic NLP (reference only)

IndicNLP and BharatRAG are research resources and are not shipped in this app. Hindi, Marathi and Konkani support here is limited to a small keyword list, multilingual embeddings, and translated result headlines. The Marathi and Konkani wording needs native-speaker review.

Private records and contacts (used)

Stored only in this browser's storage on this phone. Encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 310,000 rounds) through the Web Crypto API. There is no account, server or recovery: a forgotten passphrase means the data cannot be read. Limits: clearing browser data deletes it, anyone who learns your passphrase can read it, and a compromised phone can leak it, so keep encrypted backups. This is not a certified medical record system. Messaging your support person or sharing a summary is always your own action. The SMS contains only the urgency colour and, if you tick it, a location link.

Speech (browser)

Web Speech API for input and speech synthesis for the Listen button. Konkani uses Hindi (hi-IN) recognition and a Hindi voice to read Konkani text. Quality may vary.
